2. What Dapster is, and is not

Dapster is a persona app. On Dapster, every member creates and plays a persona: a character with any adult age, any gender, any personality, or even an animal. Everyone on Dapster knows that profiles are personas, not real identities. That is the whole idea: real people, imagined personas.

  • Real people: every account must belong to one real adult aged 18 or over who has passed our automatic selfie check.
  • Imagined personas: the name, age shown, bio, story and details on a profile are fiction chosen by the member. Nothing on a profile is a statement of fact about the person behind it, and we do not check it.
Dapster is not: a dating service, a matchmaking or introduction service, an escort service, or a service where anyone is paid for company, companionship or sex. We do not promise you a match, a chat, a reply, a meeting or a relationship.

Dapster is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any other product or company that uses a similar name (for example, products called "Dapster AI").

6. How Dapster works

Feed and visibility

  • Your feed shows members of the opposite gender who have completed the selfie check. There are no age or distance filters; your approximate location affects the order.
  • New male members see a set of profiles chosen by our team during their first 24 hours, then profiles in random order.
  • Women's feeds are ordered in tiers: members online now, then King members, then boosted members, then new members, then everyone else. Within each tier the order is shuffled.
  • Paying changes visibility. Paid boosts and the King plan move a profile higher in women's feeds. This is advertised visibility, not a sign of quality or trust.
  • The "online" mark is shown only if the member was actually active in the last 10 minutes and has not switched availability off.
  • Men's profiles show a plan badge (Pro, Max or King) to women viewing them.

Likes, matches and requests

  • You like a profile. If both of you like each other, that is a match and you can chat.
  • Free male members can send 10 likes a day.
  • Max and King members can send an instant message to a profile without a match.
  • You can find a member by their exact Dapster ID and send them a request. Requests are open for 24 hours.
  • You can send up to 5 friend requests a day.

Chats

  • Chats support text, view-once photos (5, 7 or 10 second timer), voice notes, listen-once audio, location sharing and games.
  • Match and friend chats disappear 24 hours after each message is sent. The last 5 messages of a chat may stay for up to 7 days, as text only.
  • In instant-message and Dapster ID request chats, either person may switch disappearing messages off. Those messages then stay until the chat or an account is deleted.
  • Sharing your location in a chat sends your approximate position to the other person.

Free limits

  • Women: Dapster is free, with no chat limits.
  • Free men: your first two matches get a limited message window. After that you can open one chat per day with limited messages, and send at most 10 messages per day. Each chat can receive up to 14 messages; after that, new messages in that chat stay locked until you buy a plan.
  • We may change free limits. Changes are shown in the app.

Channels

  • Anyone can ask to create a channel. Our team approves or declines each request.
  • Only King members can post in channels. Posts appear under a random anonymous name. Anonymous means anonymous to other members only: our team can see who wrote each post.
  • Posts can have several pages and may be "read once".
  • Paid channels need a channel pass. Men also need an active plan. Without access you can see text previews only; images are hidden and audio plays a 30-second preview.
  • King members can message a post's author privately ("post messages"). Swear words are masked in channels, and mentions of WhatsApp, Telegram or Snapchat are blocked in post messages.
  • "Dapster Info" is our official one-way channel for announcements. Nobody can reply to it.

7. Persona rules

Personas are fun because everyone is in on it. These rules keep it that way.

  • Personas are fiction inside Dapster only. Do not use your persona to deceive anyone outside the app, or to make someone believe something false that harms them.
  • No money, gifts or personal details through a persona. Do not use a persona to ask for money, gifts, loans, investments, crypto, bank or card details, passwords, ID documents or other real personal information.
  • No cheating or fraud by pretending to be someone. Cheating by personation and identity theft are crimes under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (sections 66C and 66D) and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (sections 318 and 319).
  • No child personas. Zero tolerance for sexualising minors. Every persona must be an adult. Profiles, posts and chats must never describe, role-play or sexualise anyone under 18. Our text filter blocks profile and channel text that describes a minor. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • No impersonating real, identifiable people, including celebrities, public figures and private individuals, and no using other people's photos.
  • No sexually explicit content and no sexual services. Publishing or sending obscene or sexually explicit material is an offence under sections 67 and 67A of the IT Act. Do not offer, ask for or advertise sex, escort services or paid companionship.
  • No intimate images without consent. Never share, or threaten to share, intimate or nude images of anyone without their consent, including edited or AI-generated ones.
  • Anyone can step out of character at any time. Any member can drop the persona, set a boundary, say no, unmatch or block, whenever they want and without giving a reason. Respect it.

9. Safety tools, moderation and appeals

Your tools

  • Report: on profile cards, in chats, on channel posts, on whole channels and on post messages. You can choose a reason, including a child-safety reason.
  • Block: on profile cards, in chats and on post messages. Blocking deletes the chat history between the two of you. You can see and undo blocks in Settings.
  • Unmatch and mute are also available.

How we moderate

  • Automatic scanning: every text message, caption and channel post is automatically scanned for safety risks, such as phone numbers, mentions of other platforms, requests for money, and violence or blackmail. Flagged text is stored for review.
  • Staff access: authorised Dapster staff may access conversations for safety, moderation, legal compliance and to handle reports.
  • Photos are not scanned automatically. They are reviewed when reported or during our own reviews.
  • Moderation can miss content. If you see something wrong, please report it.

What we can do

If you break these Terms or the law, we may, depending on how serious it is: warn you; restrict your chat for up to 90 days; remove photos, posts or other content; ban you for a set time or permanently; or delete your account. For serious harm (for example child safety, threats, or fraud) we may act without warning and may report to the authorities.

Appeals

If you think we made a wrong decision about your content or account, write to our Grievance Officer (Section 19) with your Dapster ID and what happened. If you are not satisfied with the Grievance Officer's decision, you can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee (see Section 19).
