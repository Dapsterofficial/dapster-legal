Delete Your Dapster Account

Last updated: 9 September 2026

You can delete your Dapster account at any time, either from inside the app or by emailing us. You do not need the app installed to request deletion.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Dapster and go to your Profile tab.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email dapsterofficial@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, or include your Dapster ID, with the subject line "Delete my account".

We aim to action requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before we proceed, so that nobody can delete an account that is not theirs.

What happens to your data

We want to be accurate rather than reassuring, so here is exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

DataWhat happens
Your messages and conversationsPermanently deleted
Your matches and likesPermanently deleted
Your profile visibilityRemoved immediately — other members can no longer find, see, or contact you
Your profile details and photosRetained in our systems, hidden from all members
Your verification selfieRetained unless you ask us to delete it
Purchase and payment recordsRetained — we are required to keep these for accounting, tax and refund purposes
Moderation and safety recordsRetained where we need them to keep other members safe
Routine backupsCopies may persist in backups for a period after deletion
Please read this part. Deleting your account does not erase everything about you from our systems. We retain your profile record, your photos, your verification image, and your purchase history, as set out above. If you want your profile details, photos or verification selfie erased as well, email dapsterofficial@gmail.com and ask for full erasure, and we will delete them.

Why some data is kept

  • Safety. If an account was reported or banned, deleting every trace would let the same person return immediately and repeat the behaviour against other members.
  • Money. Purchase records support refunds, chargebacks, and our tax and accounting obligations.
  • Legal. We may be required to retain certain records to comply with applicable law.

Premium plans

Dapster premium plans are one-time 30-day purchases, not subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel. If you delete your account while a plan is active, the remaining days are forfeited and are not refunded by us. Refunds are handled by Google Play under their refund policy.
