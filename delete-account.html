Delete your Dapster account

Effective: 29 September 2026 · Version 7.0

This page explains how to delete your account in the app Dapster: Play Any Persona (Android package com.dapster.app), published on Google Play by the Dapster developer, and exactly what happens to your data.

What changed in 7.0
  • Deletion now permanently erases your profile, photos, private photos, selfie check and remaining messages within 30 days. Earlier versions kept your profile and photos after deletion; that is no longer the case.
  • New in-app path: Profile > ⋮ > Delete account.
  • We now list exactly what is kept (purchase records and a one-way hashed copy of your login identifiers) and for how long.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Dapster and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You are logged out and your profile is hidden straight away.

Option 2: Ask by email (if you cannot use the app)

  1. Email dapsterofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your Dapster account, or include your Dapster ID.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours, so nobody can delete someone else's account.
  4. We delete the account within 30 days of your request. The same steps as below then apply.

Messages you sent that the other person saved or captured outside Dapster are outside our control.

Accounts deleted by Dapster (bans)

If we delete an account for breaking our rules, the same deletion steps apply. The hashed identifiers are kept so the person cannot register again.

Paid plans

Plans and passes are one-time Google Play purchases with no auto-renewal, so there is nothing to cancel. Deleting your account ends any paid time left. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

Only want to remove some data?

  • You can delete photos and edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • You can withdraw location consent in Settings > Privacy & consent; this clears your stored location.
  • For anything else, email the Grievance Officer (below).
